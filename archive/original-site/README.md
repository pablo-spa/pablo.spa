# Original pablo.spa site content archive

Source: https://github.com/Smartcolorbomb/pablosite
Archived locally on 2026-10-04.

This folder preserves the site's human-authored information and uploaded media before changing its publishing stack. The original Hugo/Netlify deployment was not modified.

## Preserved

- `content/en/` and `content/es/`: original English and Spanish home, Now, admin, and post Markdown files, including their front matter and dates.
- `uploads/`: the three original uploaded images (`img_5685.jpeg`, `profile.jpg`, and `profile750.jpg`).

## Content notes for the redesign

- The site was configured in English and Spanish.
- The `Now` pages are dated 2025-08-02 and describe a temporary stay in the Swiss Alps. Treat them as historical until Pablo supplies a current update.
- The English post explains why Pablo started the blog: sharing meaningful life updates, practicing writing, learning web development, and keeping a small personal presence on his own domain.
- The Spanish post file currently contains English body text. It has been preserved unchanged; it needs a Spanish translation before being presented as a Spanish-language post.
- `content/en/admin.md` and `content/es/admin.md` are redirects to the old CMS admin route. They are included for completeness, not as user-facing profile content.

The archive is source material. It is not a configured Hugo site and is not connected to a deployment service.
