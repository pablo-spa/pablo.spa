A little about me

I like understanding how things work, then making them work better.

At a family furniture workshop in Oaxaca, I worked across production, purchasing and logistics. Helping the team make its information and everyday processes easier to manage led me to build internal digital tools, including an operations system used across the business.

I studied mechanical engineering with an energy focus. These days I’m based in Hamburg, still drawn to practical problems at the intersection of people, systems and technology.

B.Eng. Mechanical Engineering · further coursework in Industrial Engineering
