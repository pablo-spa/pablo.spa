A little context

I learned to build by first understanding how the work happens.

For more than three years, I worked across production coordination, purchasing, logistics and digital projects at Carpintería Huayapam, a family furniture workshop in Oaxaca. I saw how disconnected spreadsheets and paper records made everyday decisions harder.

I started with the information itself: making records consistent, mapping the work with the people doing it, then helping develop an internal system that connected quoting, projects, purchasing, inventory and production. The point was never software for its own sake. It was giving a team clearer information and a more workable process.

That experience brought together things I enjoy: understanding a system from the ground up, spotting where it gets stuck, and making a practical improvement. My engineering studies gave me a way to think about systems; working alongside a team taught me what makes a solution usable.

Operations · Oaxaca, Mexico

Making workshop operations visible

Helped move a furniture workshop from scattered spreadsheets and paper records toward connected information and a home-grown operations system.
